General Practioners see a large number of patients with skin lesions everyday, but early melanomas can sometimes be hard to spot from harmless moles. Moreover in many countries hospitals and clinics are overloaded with patients (e.g. The UK) thus it is important that high risk patients get referred for an urgent appointment, while low risk stay in primary care.

This tool acts as a triage engine for primary care. It helps physicians examine lesions based on standardized clinical rules and categorizes patients into to those that require urgent patients and those that must stay in primary care.

This tool relies on NICE NG12 Guidelines and the Weighted 7-Point Checklist.
For major structural features ike changes in size, irregular borders, or irregular color—carry higher statistical odds ratios for melanoma, thus they are weighted at 2 points.
For Secondary Symptoms like like diameter over 7mm or itching—carry 1 point.
And if a patient scores more than 3 they are referred for an urgent appointment.
