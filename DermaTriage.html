Skin Lesion Risk Assessment

A structured questionnaire designed to identify skin lesion features that may warrant further professional evaluation.

Lesion Assessment

Answer all seven questions below.

1. Has the lesion changed in size?

2. Is the outline ragged, blurred, notched, or asymmetrical rather than smooth and round?

3. Does the mole have multiple shades, patchy distribution, or uneven pigmentation?

4. Is the largest diameter of the lesion 7 mm or greater?

5. Is there a red border or swelling surrounding the lesion?

6. Has the lesion bled, oozed, or formed a crust/scab?

7. Is there new or persistent itching, tingling, or tenderness?
